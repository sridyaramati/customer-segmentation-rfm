An end-to-end data analytics project that processes raw e-commerce transaction data in **MySQL**, performs **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) quintile scoring**, and translates customer behavior into an executive-ready **Power BI** dashboard focused on **Revenue Security & Churn Risk**.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Understanding customer value goes beyond basic revenue totals. This project segments **4,335 unique customers** into behavioral cohorts to answer a core business question:

> **"Where is our revenue coming from today, and how much of it is at risk of churning?"**

**Dataset used:** [Online Retail II](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vijayuv/onlineretail) — UK-based online retail transactions.

---

## 💡 Key Insights Uncovered

| Insight | Detail |
|---|---|
| 🏆 **Champions Drive Growth** | 34.7% ($4.51K) of total revenue is generated by top-tier Champions |
| ⚠️ **Revenue At Risk** | $3.38K (43.08% of customer revenue share) sits in at-risk or churning segments (*About To Sleep, At Risk, Lost*) — requiring immediate re-engagement campaigns |
| 📈 **Growth Potential** | $2.89K (22.23%) belongs to Loyal and Potential Loyalists — prime upsell opportunities |

## 🔄 Data Pipeline Architecture

```
[ Raw Transaction Data ]
           │
           ▼
[ MySQL Data Processing ]
  ├── Aggregate Customer Totals (R, F, M)
  ├── Set Dynamic Anchor Date (MAX(invoice_date) + 1 day)
  ├── Apply NTILE(5) Quantiles (Scores 1 to 5)
  └── Map Scores to 8 Business Segments
           │
           ▼
[ Power BI Dashboard & DAX Layer ]
  ├── Modeled Key Metrics (Total Revenue, Revenue Share %)
  ├── Segment Classification & Revenue Health Categorization
  └── Built Interactive 5x5 RFM Matrix & Heatmap
```
